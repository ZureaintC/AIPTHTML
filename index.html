FAQ

How does AIPT work?

AIPT provides an API that any AI agent (ChatGPT, Claude, etc.) can use to create personalized training plans. The app handles workout execution, timing, and tracking — while AI handles the planning.

Do I need an account?

No. AIPT works entirely on-device. All your training data is stored locally on your iPhone. No registration or cloud account required.

Does Apple Watch work without iPhone?

Currently, Apple Watch acts as a companion to your iPhone. It needs to be paired with an iPhone running AIPT to display and control workouts.

Which AI agents are supported?

Any AI agent that can make HTTP API calls — including ChatGPT (via Actions/GPTs), Claude (via MCP/tools), custom bots, and more. AIPT provides a local API endpoint for plan submission.

What languages are supported?

AIPT supports 17 languages: English, 简体中文, 繁體中文, 日本語, 한국어, Español, Français, Deutsch, Italiano, Português (BR), Русский, العربية, Čeština, Magyar, Polski, Türkçe, Español (Latinoamérica).

Privacy Policy

AIPT respects your privacy. Here's what you need to know:

  • All training data is stored locally on your device
  • No user accounts or cloud storage required
  • Health data (weight, height, heart rate) is accessed through Apple HealthKit with your explicit permission
  • AIPT does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data
  • The local API server only accepts connections from your device's network
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs are included

Your data stays on your device. Period.
