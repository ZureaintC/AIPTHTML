AIPT Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Overview

AIPT ("AI Personal Trainer") is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains how the app handles your information.

In short: AIPT does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

Data Storage

All data generated by AIPT is stored locally on your iOS device using Apple's SwiftData framework. This includes:

  • Training plans received from AI agents
  • Workout records and performance history
  • Personal profile information (name, age, training preferences)
  • App settings and preferences

No data is uploaded to any external server, cloud service, or third-party platform.

Apple HealthKit

AIPT integrates with Apple HealthKit to:

  • Read: Body weight, height, biological sex, date of birth, and heart rate data
  • Write: Workout records and body measurements

HealthKit access requires your explicit permission. You can grant or revoke access at any time through iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Health. AIPT never accesses HealthKit data without your consent.

Local API Server

AIPT includes a local HTTP API server that allows AI agents to submit training plans. This server:

  • Runs only on your device's local network
  • Is only active when the app is open
  • Does not expose any data to the internet
  • Accepts training plan data and serves training history for AI optimization

Apple Watch

When paired with an Apple Watch, AIPT uses Apple's WatchConnectivity framework to sync training state between your iPhone and Watch. This communication happens directly between your paired devices and does not involve any external servers.

No Third-Party Services

AIPT does not include:

  • Analytics or tracking SDKs
  • Advertising frameworks
  • Crash reporting services that transmit data externally
  • Social media integrations

No Account Required

AIPT does not require user registration, login, or any form of account creation. The app is fully functional without providing any personal identifiers.

Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete all AIPT data by:

  • Using the "Clear All Data" option in Settings within the app
  • Uninstalling the app from your device

Children's Privacy

AIPT does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The app does not require any personal information to function.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
